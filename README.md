# reCOVERme
![reCOVERme](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/blob/main/reCOVERme%20logo/Banner.png)

I want to provide the info needed for people to know that their data is safe.

It seems like a daunting task to keep track of all of your data. Most of us don't even realize how much data we are actually responsible for.

I want to help people to understand what their digital footprint is and how to safeguard it, whether that be creating backups, monitoring your exposure, or locking down your accounts. 

Contained in this repo is all of the information that I have provided to friends and family over the years, including the documents and tools that I use myself, both personally and when working on their behalf.

## Contents:
[DMCA kit](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/tree/main/DMCA%20kit) - Everything you need to file a DCMA.

[account recovery](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/tree/main/account%20recovery) - Identify as complete a list as possible of accounts you have online. This is crucial in identifying areas bad actors can exploit, account sthat need to be terminated, and accounts that need to be made secure. A PDF copy is uploaded for quick review. Download a copy of .xlsx spreadsheet to complete the worksheet.

[app data backup menu](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/tree/main/app%20data%20backup%20menu) - Use this document to identify what data is your top priority. Which areas do you think need the most attention? 

[backup vs sync infographic](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/tree/main/backup%20vs%20sync%20infographic) - What's the difference between a backup and a sync?

[family backup docs](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/tree/main/family%20backup%20docs) - These templates will help you get your digital affairs in order in the event you can no longer maintain your accounts.

[service tiers toolkit](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/tree/main/service%20tiers%20toolkit) - This worksheet serves as a good reference for making a plan of action for securing your digital footprint.

[setup process](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/tree/main/setup%20process) - This is what I use when I am getting started on a service project.
