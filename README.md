# reCOVERme
![reCOVERme](https://github.com/wumwumwum/reCOVERme/blob/main/reCOVERme%20logo/Banner.png)

I want to provide the info needed for people to know that there data is safe.

It seems like a daunting task to keep track of all of your data. Most of us don't even realize how much data we are actually responsible for.

I want to help people to understand what their digital footprint is and how to safeguard it, whether that be creating backups, monitoring your exposure, or locking down your accounts. 

Contained in this repo is all of the information that I have provided to friends and family over the years, including the documents and tools that I use myself, both personally and when working on their behalf.
